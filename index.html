Finish one clean loop before you grind.

The tutorial already shows the economy. Follow it once, then use wall break time—not guesswork—to decide when to train again.

03 Progression route

Advance by decision, not by timer.

Exact costs and thresholds change. This route stays useful because it responds to the information the current game shows you.

  1. 1
    Opening

    Finish the tutorial loop once

    Train, read the wall HP bar, earn the first Win, and use it on the next dumbbell. This teaches every part of the early economy.

  2. 2
    Build

    Use walls as a readiness test

    If a wall's HP is far beyond your current pace, stop forcing it. Return to training or check whether the next dumbbell is affordable.

  3. 3
    Multiply

    Compare flat gains with Aura multipliers

    Once basic dumbbell upgrades feel expensive, compare the visible benefit of another weight with an Aura's Strength multiplier.

  4. 4
    Expand

    Move through themed areas deliberately

    Later progression includes themed areas and treadmill stations with displayed multipliers and Rebirth requirements. Read the current labels before committing.

  5. 5
    Rebuild

    Treat Rebirth as a live decision

    Rebirth is confirmed, but its exact reset contents were not. Read the current confirmation panel and rebirth only when a faster rebuild looks worthwhile.

05 FAQ

Short answers for the next decision.

Values can change; the verified relationships between Strength, walls, Wins, dumbbells, Auras, and Rebirth are the durable part.

See how facts were checked
How do I get Strength?+

Train with dumbbells. Better dumbbells improve training gains, while Auras multiply Strength.

How do I get Wins?+

Clear wall stages. The tutorial wall awards the first Win, and wall progression continues feeding the upgrade loop.

What should I buy first?+

A stronger Win-priced dumbbell is the safest early upgrade because it directly improves training. Compare the live shop values before spending.

What do Auras do?+

Auras provide Strength multipliers. They are progression upgrades, not just visual effects.

Is there Rebirth?+

Yes. Rebirth is confirmed by the tutorial, the in-game interface, and the official First Rebirth badge.

What does Rebirth reset?+

The exact reset contents were not verified reliably. Read the current in-game confirmation before using Rebirth.

Are there working codes or pets?+

No official code system, working code, or pet system was verified for this guide. We do not publish guesses.

Do I need Robux?+

Normal progression through Wins, dumbbells, walls, and Auras is visible. Optional paid boosts also exist, but this guide does not assume they are required.
